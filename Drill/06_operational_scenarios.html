The Executive Question

Capabilities that cannot operate under degraded conditions are laboratory curiosities. These four scenarios represent the operational envelope — each constrained by real-world limitations (communications denial, partial observability, edge compute). Every scenario specifies inputs, system transformations, outputs, and battlefield consequences.
