Slide 06 — Deployment & Roadmap

From Prototype to Operational Capability

Deployment Requirements

Data inputs

Multi-agent trajectory streams (position + velocity per timestep). Standard ISR formats. Optional terrain context for spatial constraints.

Compute footprint

Python-native, offline-capable. No external network calls at runtime. Scales with entity count × timesteps.

Integration path

WISDOM-aligned JSON schema (v1 contract, documented). Full decision audit trail per entity. Regime reports with observable-level explanation.

Adaptability

Role inference rules are tunable. Graph observables are parameterizable. Regime taxonomy is documented and extendable.
